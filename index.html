What is Tamil Bench? (ELI5)

[10 marks]

AI chatbots are tested on English constantly, and on Chinese, and on a handful of big European languages. Tamil — spoken by ~85 million people, official language of Tamil Nadu, Singapore and Sri Lanka — usually gets one line in a table, if it appears at all.

Tamil Bench fixes the measuring stick. We give AI models two exams, entirely in Tamil script, and publish the marks. Think of it as a report card: anyone can re-run the exam on any model via its API, and every answer sheet is saved so the scores can be checked.

If you know nothing about AI Imagine five students who claim to know Tamil. We seat them for two papers: a multiple-choice general-knowledge exam, and a reading-comprehension test where they must underline the exact phrase in a passage that answers a question. Then we tally the marks and put the topper on the board. That's this website.

MILU — the MCQ exam

பொது அறிவுத் தேர்வு

Multiple-choice questions drawn from real Indian competitive exams, in Tamil. Tests whether the model knows things in Tamil — history, law, science, agriculture.

Score = % of questions answered correctly, with 95% confidence intervals

IndicQA — the comprehension exam

வாசிப்புப் புரிதல் தேர்வு

Read a Tamil passage, answer a question using a short phrase from it — like school reading comprehension. Tests whether the model can read and quote Tamil precisely.

Score = Exact Match (did you quote the exact phrase?) and F1 (partial credit for overlapping words)

What the marks tell us

[5 × 2 = 10 marks]
  • Gemini 3.8 Flash tops Tamil MCQs decisively — 94.5%, with a confidence band that doesn't touch any other model. On exam-style knowledge in Tamil, it's a clear first.
  • Comprehension is brutally hard for everyone. Exact-match peaks at just 22%. Models clearly understand the passages — F1 scores near 40 confirm it — but they paraphrase instead of quoting the exact Tamil span, and Tamil's agglutinative endings make near-misses common.
  • Qwen is the interesting outlier: worst on MCQs (68.8%) but the best at exact-match quoting (22.0%). Knowing facts in Tamil and quoting them verbatim are different skills.
  • Rapid progress. All five models beat GPT-4o's ~74% cross-language MILU average from 2024. Two years later, four of five mid-tier "flash" models cross 79% on Tamil exam MCQs — for reference, the SEA-HELM public leaderboard's Tamil topper (SEA-LION v4) sits at 68.5 on its own, differently-scored tasks.
  • Free open-weight models fail fast. In smoke tests, several free-tier models (meta/muse-spark-1.3, nvidia/nemotron-3.5-lightning) errored or scored near zero on Tamil — cheap models still treat Tamil as second-class.

Run it yourself

[practical]

The whole bench is one Python file, no GPU, no fine-tuning: it calls models through an OpenAI-compatible API exactly the way a normal user would. Set OPENROUTER_API_KEY, then:

# Paper 1 — MILU MCQs (auto-downloads after you accept the HF gate)
python3 bench.py milu --model google/gemini-3.8-flash --n 199

# Paper 2 — IndicQA reading comprehension
python3 bench.py indicqa --model google/gemini-3.8-flash --n 100

# Re-score any saved answer sheet
python3 bench.py score --task milu --file results/milu_google_gemini-3.8-flash_n199.jsonl
