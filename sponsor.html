Sponsor Tamil Bench
தமிழ் பெஞ்சை நிதியளிப்பு

Tamil Bench runs open-weight and commercial language models through the same three Tamil exams, and publishes every score with the raw answer sheet behind it. The runs are paid for out of pocket, and this page shows exactly what they cost. Sponsor to keep the ledger open, or request a run for a model you care about.

What the benchmark has cost so far
இதுவரை செலவானதன் மொத்தம்

$1.55

OpenRouter charges across every scored run, summed from the per-answer usage.cost field in the result sheets. No salaries, no servers, no paid datasets — just API calls.

Where the money goes

  • API calls. Every paid run is metered through OpenRouter and
each answer sheet stores its own charge, so the totals above are auditable line
by line rather than estimated.

open models are run on the free tier; they expand the board at zero cost.
    •

sheet live in https://github.com/LogicIncZo/tamil-bench under an open licence. Sponsorship
buys runs and compute, not secrecy.
